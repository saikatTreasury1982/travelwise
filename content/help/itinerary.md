:::note
The co-pilot can draft a whole itinerary for you, tidy it into sections, and help with each activity. Only the plan you **finalize** feeds your Cost Forecast — so drafts and alternatives never affect your budget.
:::

## Two ways to start

The first time you open Itinerary, you choose how to begin.

- **Let AI draft it** — tell the co-pilot how you'd like the trip to feel (or just say "draft it") and it proposes a complete plan — days or stretches, activities, times, estimated costs — for you to review and edit.
- **Build it myself** — start empty and add activities yourself.

You also pick the **shape**: *Day by day* (one bucket per calendar day) or *By day-ranges* (named stretches like "Tokyo" or "Sea days"). A stretch can be a single day.

:::tip
For long trips the app recommends **day-ranges** — they keep a big trip manageable. An AI draft always lands as a draft you review; it's never finalized for you.
:::

## How the shape works

- **Day by day** — the left rail lists every day; you fill each with activities.
- **By day-ranges** — you carve the trip into named stretches. "Unplanned days" shows any span not yet covered, with **+ add a range**.

The shape is locked once you add anything. To switch, start a fresh plan while it's empty. You can rename or re-span a range inline, and reorder ranges with the ▲▼ arrows.

## Adding activities

Each activity is the thing that carries a **cost** — add as many as you like under a day or stretch.

1. Name it, and optionally add start/end times and notes.
2. Add a **cost and currency** for paid activities (leave blank for free ones). Choose **total** or **per person** (per-person × headcount = the cost).
3. Choose **who's paying** — it defaults to everyone who shares costs; deselect anyone who isn't splitting *this* activity.

:::tip
Base currency is shown everywhere; a foreign amount appears in brackets on the row — e.g. AUD 400 (JPY 200). Different activities can have different payers.
:::

## Grouping into sections

Grouping is purely for tidy reading — it never changes costs.

- **Suggest grouping** — the co-pilot reads your list and proposes sections (e.g. "Morning", "Dining"). Preview, rename, apply — or dismiss to stay flat.
- **Group manually** — make your own section and drag activities into it.

:::note
Deleting a section never deletes its activities — they simply become ungrouped again.
:::

## Complete a day — when costs count

While you're building, a day or stretch is in **planning** and its costs are **not** in your forecast — so a half-built day never skews the budget.

- Click **Complete** on a day/stretch to add its costs to your Cost Forecast (the co-pilot also offers a tidy grouping).
- **Reopen** takes it back to planning and removes its costs again.

## Finalize a plan — the one that feeds your budget

You can keep **several plans** for a trip — a draft alongside your own, or two alternatives. Only one feeds the forecast.

- A plan you're just comparing has no effect on your budget.
- **Finalize** the plan you're going with — its completed days' costs flow to the forecast, and any previously-finalized plan steps down.

:::note
Finalizing is always your choice — the AI never finalizes for you. With one plan, the first you build is finalized automatically; the choice only appears once you have two or more.
:::

## Two ways to view

Switch anytime with the **List / Timeline** control.

- **List** — the full working view: add, edit, group, complete. Best for building.
- **Timeline** — a calm visual read of the day: activities on a spine, coloured by section, times where you set them, and each cost. If every activity has a time it sorts chronologically; otherwise it keeps your order.

At the top, a collapsible **Spend by day** chart shows how your activity spend spreads across days or stretches, with the biggest highlighted and a one-line insight. Tap a segment to jump to that day.

## Activity Assist — ask the co-pilot, save for the trip

Tap **✨** on any activity for practical help, grounded in *your* trip — it knows your dates, travellers, and even your booked hotel.

- **How do I get here?** — routes from your stay (train/taxi, rough time and fare).
- **Food nearby** · **How long / best time** · **Tips**.
- **Ask** — anything else, in a short chat.

Like an answer? **Save** it — it becomes a brief note on that activity. Save several per type if you like (e.g. two food options).

:::tip
During the trip, tap ✨ to read your saved notes instantly — they work **offline**, no signal needed. A small ✨ count on an activity shows it has saved help. Answers are practical estimates, not live data.
:::

## How itinerary affects your cost forecast

Your forecast always shows the best-known cost right now.

- A **planning** day or stretch is not in the forecast.
- A **completed** day or stretch, in the **finalized** plan, counts each active, priced activity with a payer — split among its payers.
- Non-finalized plans have no effect until you finalize them.

## Common questions

### My costs aren't showing in the forecast — why?
Two things must both be true: the plan is **finalized**, and the day or stretch is **completed**. Also check the activity has a cost, a currency, and at least one payer.

### An activity has a payer but shows no cost.
Only priced activities count — a payer alone doesn't add anything to the budget.

### I picked day-by-day for a long trip and it's unwieldy.
Day-ranges suit long trips. Start a fresh plan and choose By day-ranges.

### Can I keep two versions of my plan?
Yes — build as many as you like and finalize the one you're going with. The others stay harmless drafts.

### Do my saved assist notes affect the budget?
No. Activity Assist notes are reference only — never financial.

### Did grouping delete my activities?
No. Deleting a section only ungroups its activities; they're never lost.

### How do I start over and re-pick the shape?
**Delete & start over** removes the whole plan (its costs leave the forecast). If it was your only plan, you're returned to the day/range choice to begin fresh.
