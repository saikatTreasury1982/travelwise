## How a flight moves

Every flight is in one of three stages; the same record carries through, so nothing is re-entered.

:::lifecycle:::

## The three ways to add a flight

You never type a flight from scratch — each method brings the details in for you.

:::doors:::

## Getting AI flight suggestions

1. On the Flights page, click **Suggest flights with AI** and choose Return or One-way.
2. The co-pilot asks which city or airport you're flying from (and which destination, if your trip has several).
3. It proposes 3–4 options — a cheaper one, a convenient one, a premium one — each with an estimated fare, duration, and stops.
4. Want different options? Ask — "show me the fastest and cheapest", "only direct flights" — and it re-suggests.
5. Click **Shortlist** on the options you want to keep.

:::tip
Fares are estimates ("est."), not live quotes — you'll set the real price when you book. If a city has no airport of its own, the co-pilot uses the nearest major one and tells you.
:::

## Uploading a booking you already made

1. Drag your booking PDF onto the drop zone at the top of the Flights page.
2. The co-pilot reads it and opens a review screen; anything it was unsure about is highlighted.
3. Check the details and fill in anything missing — for instance, a price the document didn't show.
4. Assign who's paying, then save. The flight lands as **Booked** straight away.

:::warn
If the upload matches a flight you already planned, Travelwise offers to update *that* flight instead of creating a duplicate.
:::

## Confirming & assigning who pays

Choosing a flight puts it in your budget, so Travelwise needs to know who's sharing the cost.

- Only **cost-sharing travellers** appear in the payer picker.
- The fare **splits equally** among the payers you select.
- You can change the payers later from the flight's card.

:::note
Confirming is not the same as booking: confirming means "this is the flight I'm going with" — it becomes a Planned flight at its estimated price. Booking comes later, when you've paid.
:::

## Marking a planned flight as booked

1. On a Planned flight's card, click **I've confirmed this**.
2. **Upload confirmation** — drop the booking PDF and the co-pilot fills in the real price, reference, and times; or
3. **Enter price manually** — booked by phone or agent? Just type the real price you paid.

The flight becomes **Booked**, and its real price replaces the estimate in your forecast. A small variance chip shows how your estimate compared to what you paid.

## How flights affect your cost forecast

Your forecast always shows the best-known cost right now.

- **Considering** flights are not in the forecast.
- **Planned** flights count at their estimated price.
- **Booked** flights count at their real price — updated automatically.

## Warnings & common questions

### Why was I warned about a duplicate flight?
Confirming a flight on the same route and date as one you've already confirmed — shared with the same payer — gives you a heads-up in case it's a double-entry. It never blocks you.

### Travelwise asked to change my trip dates — why?
A flight outside your trip's current dates offers to stretch the trip dates to fit — a real booking is the reliable fact. Accept, or keep your dates and save anyway.

### Can I move a flight back a stage?
A Planned flight from an AI suggestion can go back to Shortlisted with "Move to shortlist" — it leaves your forecast until you confirm again. Uploaded and booked flights can't (they're real).

### How do I edit or remove a flight?
Every flight card has Edit booking and a delete button. Deleting removes its cost from your forecast. Click a flight's leg list to edit it.

### Do you keep my uploaded PDF?
No. The co-pilot reads the details out and discards the file — only the extracted flight details are saved.

### Why can't I add a flight to my trip?
You need at least one cost-sharing traveller on the trip, plus a price and currency. If the payer picker is empty, add a cost-sharing traveller in *Who's going* first.
