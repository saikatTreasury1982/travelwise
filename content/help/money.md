:::note
This is the heart of Travelwise. Every other part of the app — flights, lodging, your itinerary, extra costs — feeds one live budget picture, so you always know where you stand: before the trip, and during it. No spreadsheet, no adding things up by hand.
:::

## The idea in one minute

As you plan, Travelwise keeps a **Cost Forecast** — a running estimate of what the trip will cost, in your home currency, split across the people sharing costs. Then, on the road, you record what you **actually** spend, and Travelwise shows you the **variance** — how reality compares to the plan.

- **Forecast** = what you expect to pay, best-known right now.
- **Actuals** = what you really paid.
- **Variance** = the difference, so nothing surprises you.

The forecast isn't a number you maintain — it builds itself from the choices you make in each module.

## How the forecast builds itself

Every module feeds the same forecast automatically:

- Confirm a **flight** → its fare joins the forecast.
- Confirm a **stay** → its cost joins.
- Complete a day in your **itinerary** → those activity costs join.
- Add an **extra cost** → it joins.

You never press a "calculate" button — the forecast updates the moment you confirm something. Remove or un-confirm an item and it drops straight back out.

:::tip
The forecast shows **committed** costs only — things you've actually decided on. A hotel you're still weighing up (shortlisted, not confirmed) isn't counted, so the number reflects real decisions, not maybes.
