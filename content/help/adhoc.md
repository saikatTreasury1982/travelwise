:::note
Some trip costs don't fit flights, lodging, or a specific activity — travel insurance, visas, a SIM card, airport parking, gifts. Add those here so your Cost Forecast is complete.
:::

## Adding an expense

1. On the Ad-hoc Expenses page, click **Add expense**.
2. Give it a **name** (e.g. "Travel insurance"), an **amount and currency**, and pick a **category** (Transport, Food, Insurance, and so on).
3. Choose **who's paying** from the trip's cost-sharing travellers — the cost splits equally among them.
4. Optionally add a **date** and a **note**, then save.

The expense is added to your forecast straight away.

:::tip
Base currency is shown as the main figure; if you enter a foreign amount, the original is shown alongside so nothing is hidden.
:::

## Paying in a different currency

Entered a cost in another currency? Travelwise converts it to your home currency for the forecast.

- Enter the amount in **whatever currency it's in** — the app shows what it adds to your forecast.
- If you know the exact home-currency amount your card was charged, you can **enter that too** — Travelwise uses your real figure instead of a mid-market rate, so the forecast matches your statement.

## Active vs inactive

Every expense has an **Active / Inactive** switch.

- **Active** expenses count towards your Cost Forecast.
- **Inactive** expenses are kept for reference but left out of the forecast — handy for a "maybe" cost you don't want to commit to yet.

## Splitting between travellers

- Only **cost-sharing travellers** appear in the picker (non-payers like children aren't listed).
- The amount **splits equally** among the travellers you select.
- A live preview shows the per-person share as you assign it.

## How ad-hoc costs affect your forecast

Every **active** ad-hoc expense counts towards your Cost Forecast at its amount, split among its assigned travellers. Inactive ones don't count until you switch them back on. Edit or remove any expense from its row — removing it takes its cost out of the forecast.

## Common questions

### What belongs here vs the other modules?
Anything not tied to a flight, a stay, or a specific itinerary activity — insurance, visas, connectivity, parking, tips, gifts, and other one-off costs.

### Why can't I save an expense?
You need a name, a valid amount, a currency, and at least one cost-sharing traveller. If the traveller picker is empty, add a cost-sharing traveller in *Who's going* first.

### I don't want a cost in my budget yet — do I have to delete it?
No — switch it to **Inactive**. It stays in the list for reference but is left out of the forecast until you reactivate it.
