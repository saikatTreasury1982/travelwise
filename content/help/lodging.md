:::note
Hotels often don't give you a document — many bookings are just a confirmation number or an email. So lodging isn't built around uploads: the co-pilot suggests places, you shortlist and confirm, then book with the provider and mark it booked (by reference, or by uploading a confirmation if you have one).
:::

## How a stay moves

Every stay is in one of three stages; the same record carries through, so nothing is re-entered.

- **Considering** — options you're weighing up. Not in your forecast.
- **Planned** — the stay you've chosen, at an estimated price. In your forecast.
- **Booked** — really booked, at the real price. Your forecast updates to what you paid.

A Planned stay suggested by AI can step back to Considering. A booked stay is final.

## The ways to add a stay

You don't type a stay from scratch — start from an AI suggestion, then book it externally.

- **Suggest places to stay** *(available)* — the co-pilot proposes options per destination with estimated nightly rates.
- **Search links** *(available)* — on a Planned or confirmed stay, jump to **Google Hotels** or **Booking.com** with the name and dates prefilled, to book with the provider.
- **Search hotels in-app** *(coming soon)* — browse and book without leaving Travelwise.

## Getting AI lodging suggestions

1. On the Lodging page, click **Suggest places to stay**.
2. The co-pilot asks your tentative **check-in and check-out** dates (and which destination, if your trip has several).
3. It proposes a few options — a value one, a comfortable one, a premium one — each with an estimated nightly rate and a total for the stay.
4. Click **Shortlist** on the ones you want to keep; they appear under **Considering** to compare.

:::tip
Rates are estimates, not live quotes — you'll set the real price when you book. Both a nightly rate and the total for the stay are shown, so you can see the whole cost at a glance.
:::

## Confirming a stay & who pays

Choosing a stay puts it in your budget, so Travelwise needs the dates and who's sharing the cost.

1. On a shortlisted stay, click **Confirm**.
2. Set the **check-in / check-out** dates (nights are worked out for you) and, if it's for one destination among several, its destination.
3. Pick **who's paying** from the trip's cost-sharing travellers — the cost splits equally among them.

:::note
Confirming is not the same as booking: confirming means "this is the stay I'm going with" — it becomes a **Planned** stay at its estimated price. Booking comes later, when you've actually reserved it.
:::

## Booking, and marking it booked

Once you've confirmed a stay, use the **Google Hotels** or **Booking.com** link on its card to book with the provider. Then record it:

1. On the Planned stay, click **I've booked this**.
2. Enter the **real price paid** and, optionally, a **confirmation reference** — or **upload a confirmation** if the provider gave you one and let the co-pilot read the details.

The stay becomes **Booked**, and its real price replaces the estimate in your forecast.

## Paying in a different currency

Booked a stay in the local currency? Travelwise handles the conversion so your forecast stays in your home currency.

- Enter the price in **whatever currency you paid** — the app converts it and shows what it adds to your forecast.
- If your card statement shows the exact home-currency amount, you can **enter that too** — Travelwise uses your real figure instead of a mid-market rate, so the forecast matches your statement.

:::tip
The forecast always shows amounts in your home currency; the original foreign amount is shown alongside so nothing is hidden.
:::

## Nights coverage

At the top of the Lodging page, a **nights-covered** indicator shows how many of your trip's nights have a confirmed stay — and flags any nights still unbooked, so gaps don't slip through.

## How lodging affects your cost forecast

Your forecast always shows the best-known cost right now.

- **Considering** stays are not in the forecast.
- **Planned** stays count at their estimated price.
- **Booked** stays count at the real price you paid — updated automatically.

The trip's Lodging card shows "N confirmed · M shortlisted".

## Common questions

### Why does lodging work differently from flights?
Flights almost always come with a document to upload; hotels often don't. So lodging leads with AI suggestions and lets you book with a provider and record it by reference — with document upload as an option, not the main path.

### Can I move a stay back a stage?
A Planned stay suggested by AI can go back to Considering — it leaves your forecast until you confirm again. A booked stay is final.

### Why can't I add a stay to my trip?
Confirming a stay needs check-in/out dates, at least one cost-sharing traveller, and a price with a currency. If the payer picker is empty, add a cost-sharing traveller in *Who's going* first.

### Do you keep my uploaded confirmation?
No. If you upload one, the co-pilot reads the details out and discards the file — only the extracted details are saved.

### The search links didn't book my stay automatically.
The Google Hotels and Booking.com links take you to the provider to book; Travelwise can't see that booking yet, so mark it booked afterwards (by price/reference or upload). Booking inside the app is a future feature.
